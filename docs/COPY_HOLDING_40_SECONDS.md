# Research holding threshold: 40 seconds

The owner explicitly lowered the research selection threshold from 60 to 40
seconds on 23 September 2026. The metric remains the unweighted median between
each confirmed acquisition lot and its first FIFO partial sale. Open lots are
censored. Incomplete or ambiguous histories remain unknown and ineligible.

This changes only the research holding gate and the default of offline copy
reports. ROI >=20%, average operations/day <=50, dated observed funds >=10 USD,
and infrastructure exclusions remain required for the reviewed shortlist.
No promotion, safety scoring, paper selection, live authority or activation is
granted. A median of 40 seconds does not establish sub-40-second copy latency,
execution profitability, or a minimum duration for every trade.

Prior reports and source histories remain preserved. Updated reports recompute
both eligibility and the fraction of exits below 40 seconds from saved decoded
transactions, without refreshing Nansen or spending credits.
